How to play

  • Every card has four numbers.
  • Use all four, each one once, with + − × ÷ to make 24.
  • No sticking digits together: a 1 and a 2 can’t become 12.

Two ways to play

  • Tap: tap a number, tap a sign, tap another number. They join into a new number. Keep going until one card says 24!
  • Type: use the keypad to type the whole thing, like (7 − 8 ÷ 8) × 4, then tap Check it!

Pairs that make 24

3 × 84 × 62 × 1212 + 1220 + 4
  • Try to build a 3, 4, 6 or 8 from two numbers, then multiply by what you make from the other two.

Stars and hints

  • Solve a card for up to 3 stars. Solve cards in a row to grow your streak.
  • Stuck? A new hint unlocks every 20 seconds. Each hint you use costs one star, but hints help you learn!
  • With friends, take turns and pass the device. Most stars wins!
